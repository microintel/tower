How to Play

  • Tap a peg to lift its top disc, tap another peg to place it there.
  • Only one disc moves at a time.
  • A larger disc can never rest on a smaller one.
  • Move the whole stack from peg A to peg C.
  • Fewer moves means a cleaner solve — optimal moves = 2ⁿ − 1.
